Aurora Phoenix — a fire-phoenix bursting out of a mountain lake under vivid aurora ribbons, painted procedurally in pure Node.js (standard library only, no network, fully deterministic). One look: a white-hot burst, gold and magenta feather-plumes, a tiny watcher with a lantern on the ridge, and the burst's golden reflection in the lake. Built to make make.fun's AI judge sit up.
